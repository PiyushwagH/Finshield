## Caveats on included rows

- **nps-traders**: maandhan.in still lists the scheme and its FAQ, but I found no recent (2025-26) official update. Annual turnover (Rs 1.5 crore cap) and the income-tax-payer exclusion can't be expressed as rules.
- **post-matric-scholarship-st**: the official source is a PIB/MoTA release (the tribal.nic.in page failed to load). The `sc_st` tag also matches SC users, who have their own post-matric scheme (`nsp-post-matric`).
- **national-overseas-scholarship-sc**: the `sc_st` tag also matches ST users, who have a separate Ministry of Tribal Affairs overseas scholarship. The age, income and slot figures come from the 2026-27 MSJE scheme document.
- **karnataka-gruha-jyothi / karnataka-shakti / karnataka-yuva-nidhi / karnataka-gruha-lakshmi**: the sources are mainly 2023-24 launch coverage plus university guidelines. The schemes are reported as continuing, but I didn't find a 2026 official notice. For Yuva Nidhi, the passing-year cutoff and the 180-day unemployment wait can't be expressed as rules.
- **tn-kalaignar-magalir-urimai-thogai**: kmut.tn.gov.in had an expired TLS certificate, so the criteria come from news reports of the government's eligibility notification. Land, electricity and vehicle exclusions can't be expressed as rules.
- **maharashtra-lek-ladki**: the Rs 1 lakh income cap comes from Vikaspedia only. ThePrint (Aug 2026) confirms the yellow/orange ration card rule and the amounts, but doesn't state the income figure.
- **gujarat-namo-lakshmi**: there are no official Gujarat government pages, only news reports and Vikaspedia. `official_url` is the generic state portal because I couldn't confirm a scheme-specific official page. An admin should replace it if one exists.
- **up-old-age-pension**: the monthly amount conflicts between sources (Rs 1,000 vs Rs 1,200), so `benefit_amount` is blank. The income limits come from an official divisional page.
- **odisha-subhadra**: the income test varies between sources ("well-off families" vs < Rs 2.5 lakh), so it isn't encoded. Only the age, gender and government-employee rules are.
- **janani-suraksha-yojana**: BPL status can't be expressed as a rule. Eligibility is encoded as (female AND Low-Performing State) OR (female AND SC/ST), which leaves out BPL non-SC/ST women in High-Performing States. That under-matches rather than over-matches.
- **maharashtra-cm-vayoshri**: the official district page says the applicant must have completed 65 years "by 31 December 2023". The rule uses age >= 65, which slightly over-matches people who turned 65 after that date. The official portal URL couldn't be confirmed (the spelling differs between sources), so `official_url` is the Jalgaon district scheme page.
- **sanjay-gandhi-niradhar**: the scheme targets specific destitute categories (orphans, widows, serious illness and others). Only age < 65 and the income limit are encoded.
- In general, ration-card type, landholding size, income-tax-payer status and GST registration can't be expressed with the allowed rule fields. Where possible, rules approximate them with `employment_status not_in [salaried_government]`. Admins should keep the full criteria visible in `full_description`.
